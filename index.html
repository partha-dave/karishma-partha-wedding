The Curragh, County Kildare, Ireland · 14–15 August 2027

Martinstown House is a family-operated country house set in the leafy landscape of the Curragh in County Kildare. It's set on a fully working farmyard that houses cattle, sheep, horses and two rescue donkeys, and the entire estate is a bird sanctuary.

At the venue

Glamping

Stay right in the heart of Martinstown House in the venue's Goa to Sleep glamping village. Tucked amongst the trees, the bell tents are a fun and comfortable way to make a proper weekend of the wedding.

  • 10 fully furnished bell tents
  • Proper beds, duvets, pillows and linen provided
  • Towels and extra blankets included
  • Lighting, USB charging and a heater in each tent
  • Breakfast with the couple! (ps. probably just the Groom)

At the venue

Stables

For those who prefer four walls and a proper bedroom, the converted Stables offer a cosy place to stay right at Martinstown House, so you can enjoy the celebrations without having far to wander at the end of the night.

  • Five bedrooms in the converted stable block
  • Comfortable beds with luxury linen and duvets
  • Towels provided
  • Right at the venue, close to the celebrations
  • Breakfast with the couple! (ps. probably just the Groom)
